Deadline-Aware Planner Mechanics

How does milestone shifting work when I miss a day?
Instead of just showing a static streak, Career Navigator shifts future milestones by the exact delayed duration, calculates compression on your mock/interview phase, and provides 4 recovery buttons to pull milestones back on track.
Can I set custom timelines for Tech placements?
Yes! You can choose 3, 6, or 9+ months as your target hiring timeline, select your target difficulty tier (Product vs Service based), and specify your weekly hours and current experience level.
